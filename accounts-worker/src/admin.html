Post an update on Telegram

Post the same news in your own Telegram channel or group, in one click. One-time setup: 1. In Telegram, message @BotFather, send /newbot and copy the token it gives you. 2. Add that bot to your channel as an administrator who can post (or add it to your group). 3. Paste the token below. To pick the chat, post anything there and press Find my chats, or type the channel name like @yourchannel. To try it first, send your bot a message in a private chat and connect that.

The bot token is stored encrypted on the server and is never shown again. A post goes from the server straight to Telegram and cannot be taken back from here.
