Restarts & problems

What happens when the browser restarts, or a tab stops refreshing.

To bring a tab back, the page address and settings of each tab you start are kept on this computer only. They are never synced to your account and never sent anywhere. Pressing Stop, or closing just that tab, forgets it. The messages go to your own Telegram bot if you connected one (set it up under Needs-a-human alerts), and a desktop notice appears when something goes wrong.
On the page

What the extension does inside the page after each refresh.

Moves the mouse, scrolls a little and sometimes clicks empty space after each refresh. It helps with idle timers; it can't get past real bot protection.
The icon badge always counts down. The on-page timer is visible to the website.
Privacy & updates
Once a day the extension checks for a new version and, if this is on, sends a random install ID and the version number so the developer can count users. Never your email, settings or pages. The version alert goes to your desktop, and to your own Telegram bot if you connected one. If you write a review, its stars, text and a short name are sent and shown publicly.
