Timing

The refresh interval itself is chosen in the popup. Every reload skips the browser cache and each wait varies by a few percent, so there is nothing to set for that.

Random rangeWait a random time between two limits instead of the fixed interval, for example 250 to 300 seconds.

Active hours

The extension refreshes inside these windows and sleeps in the short gaps between them. The default is seven windows that cover the whole day with breaks of 5 to 20 minutes. Change them to whatever you like, or switch hours off to refresh around the clock.

Only during set hoursSwitch it off to refresh around the clock.
Time zoneEvery time below is read in this zone, not your computer's clock. It stays on this computer.

Restarts

What happens when the browser restarts, or a tab stops refreshing. To bring a tab back, the page address and settings of each tab you start are kept on this computer only. They are never synced to your account and never sent anywhere.

Bring my tabs back after the browser restartsPressing Stop, or closing just that tab, forgets it. Closing a whole window or quitting the browser keeps it.
Open the page again if the browser did not bring it back
Tell me when refreshing stalls, fails or comes backA message to your Telegram bot if you connected one (set it up under Alerts), and a desktop notice when something goes wrong.
